About Check

Check is a lightweight and privacy-friendly web application built to help you track and manage your expenses effortlessly. Whether you’re buying groceries, fuel, or electronics, Check makes it simple to record and analyze your purchases.

You can upload a photo of your receipt, and our system will automatically extract item details and prices using OCR technology. Over time, you’ll get a clear view of where your money goes — helping you make better financial decisions.

Check is developed with love by Dervis, combining simplicity, functionality, and modern web design. More features are coming soon — like detailed spending history and analytics.
